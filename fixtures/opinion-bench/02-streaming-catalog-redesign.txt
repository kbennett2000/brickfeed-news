Streamline Video Overhauls Its Catalog, Retiring Genre Menus for "Moods"

Streamline Video, a mid-sized regional streaming service, has redesigned its catalog
interface, replacing its long-standing genre menus with algorithmically assembled
"mood collections" with names like Rainy Sunday, Big Swings, and Comfort Food. The
company says the change reflects how subscribers actually browse, citing internal
data showing most viewers scroll past traditional genre rows.

The redesign also moves the search bar behind an extra tap and introduces a rotating
"Tonight's Lineup" screen that plays trailers automatically when the app opens.
Streamline says early testing showed subscribers who used the new home screen
started watching something 40 seconds faster on average.

Reaction from subscribers has been mixed. Longtime users have complained on the
company's forums that films they had saved are now harder to find, while others say
the mood collections surfaced titles they would never have discovered otherwise.
Streamline says classic genre browsing will remain available in a settings menu it
describes as "legacy mode."
