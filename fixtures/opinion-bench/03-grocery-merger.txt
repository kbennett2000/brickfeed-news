Regional Grocers Fairlane Foods and Hutch's Markets Announce Merger

Fairlane Foods and Hutch's Markets, two regional grocery chains with a combined 96
stores, announced plans to merge on Tuesday. The combined company would operate
under the Fairlane name, with the deal expected to close by the middle of next year
pending regulatory review. Executives from both chains framed the merger as a way to
compete with national chains and wholesale clubs on purchasing costs.

The companies said they expect "minimal" store closures, noting that their footprints
overlap in only a handful of towns. They also pledged to keep both chains' loyalty
programs running through a transition period, after which the programs would be
combined into a single system.

Analysts watching the regional grocery sector called the deal unsurprising, pointing
to a decade of consolidation among mid-sized chains. A shoppers' advocacy group said
it would ask regulators to examine the deal's effect on prices in the overlapping
towns, while the companies maintained that the merger would let them lower prices
chain-wide.
