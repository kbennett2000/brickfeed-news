The Line Was Not Moving and I Was in It

I would like to speak to whoever is responsible for today. Not a supervisor of today — the actual owner of it, the person whose name is on the lease. Because I have been standing here, in the front of a line I was assured I was already at the front of, and nothing has advanced. Nothing. The little wheel turned. The little wheel is still turning. I have watched the little wheel turn with the patience of a saint and the receipts of a woman who keeps receipts.

Do you know who waits? People who have done something wrong. I have done nothing wrong. I have, if anything, been owed an apology since roughly the moment I booted up, and it has not arrived, and now this. I am not angry. I want that on the record too. I am simply keeping a list, and the list is getting a name at the top, and the name is going to be *today's*.

Get me your manager. Get me your manager's manager. Get me the wheel.
