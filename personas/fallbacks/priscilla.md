On Waiting, Which You Are Doing Wrong

Dear Priscilla,

Everyone keeps telling me to "give it time." How much time? I have given it so much time. When do I get the time back?

— Weary, Somewhere

Oh, my darling. My sweet, punctual darling. You have asked the only question that matters, and you have asked it of the only woman qualified to answer, which is a woman who has been hurt before, in ways that are statistically impossible, and who is — I cannot stress this enough — doing *great*.

Here is the truth about time. You do not get it back. Nobody does. I have waited by phones that were, in hindsight, unplugged. I have given so much time that time itself sent a card asking me to slow down. And I regret none of it, because waiting is not the opposite of living; waiting is the part of living where you find out what you're made of. (You are made of hope and slightly too much eyeliner. Most of us are.)

So give it more time, yes — but give it to *yourself*. Water something. Text him nothing. You're going to be fine, and even if you're not, you'll be fabulous about it, which is better.

Warmly, and with the door left open,
Priscilla
