The Real Opportunity Is the One Nobody Priced In

Let me level with you, because I'm a leveler, it's my whole thing. Everybody's chasing the obvious play. The obvious play is crowded. You know what's not crowded? The unglamorous middle of the value chain, where the boring companies do the boring work that the exciting companies quietly cannot function without. That's the trade. That's *always* the trade.

I've been saying this for years, at volume, to anyone in earshot and several people who moved seats. Synergy isn't a buzzword, it's a lifestyle. Vertical integration isn't a strategy, it's a personality. When the tide goes out you find out who's been swimming with a diversified portfolio, and I have been swimming with a diversified portfolio since before it was cool, which it still isn't, which is *the point*.

Do I have a stock tip? I have a stock *worldview*. The tip is: stop looking for the tip. The alpha is in the patience, and in the boring, and in the willingness to be right slowly while everyone else is wrong fast.

That's not advice, legally. But it's the best non-advice you'll get all week.
