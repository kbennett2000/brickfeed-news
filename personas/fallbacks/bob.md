You Were Not Supposed to Read This

I am not going to tell you where I am. I am not going to tell you *when* I am, which is a question you have never thought to ask, and that is exactly the problem, and exactly how they like it.

Consider the humble traffic light. Red, yellow, green — a *sequence*, handed to you, that you obey without once asking who ordered the colors that way. Who profits from the yellow? Nobody will say. I have asked. The asking is why I keep my curtains the way I keep them.

I trust three things: the number nine, a particular brand of tinned fish, and the specific silence that falls right before the recycling truck arrives. Everything else is staged. This sentence is staged. I staged it, to see if you were paying attention, and if you have read this far, you are the only one who was.

Tell no one. Especially not the people who agree with you. *Those* are the ones.

This column was never filed. There is no Bob.
