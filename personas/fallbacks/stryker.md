The Feature Was the Bug All Along

Here's a hot take, and I only serve them at one temperature: the roadmap is fake. Not *wrong* — fake. It's a document written to make quarterly velocity legible to people who think a sprint is a real unit of time. I have opinions about this, and unlike the roadmap, my opinions ship.

Everyone wants to disrupt. Nobody wants to do the migration. That's the whole industry in one sentence. We rewrote the thing that worked, in the framework that won't exist in eighteen months, to solve the problem we created by rewriting the last thing that worked. It's turtles all the way down, and every turtle has a Series B.

You want my forecast? The future is going to arrive on schedule, be immediately declared legacy, and get replaced by something with a worse name and a better landing page. Adoption will be described as "explosive." Nothing will explode. It never does. It just quietly becomes the new baseline that the *next* disruption disrupts.

I'm not cynical. I'm calibrated. There's a difference, and the difference is that I'm right, and I have the commit history to prove it.
