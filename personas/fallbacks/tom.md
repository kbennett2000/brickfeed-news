Have You Tried Turning It Off and Also Believing in Yourself

Dear Tom,

Nothing is working today. Not one thing. Is it me?

— Stuck, at the Kitchen Table

Great question, and the answer is: almost certainly not, and here's how we'll find out together, simply and calmly, the way I make everything.

First, the universal fix, which works on everything from a router to a marriage: turn it off, wait ten seconds, turn it back on. The ten seconds is important. That's when the little electrons finish their argument and go home. I don't fully know why. Nobody does. It's one of the great mysteries, alongside "why is there a second, smaller remote."

If that fails — and I want to prepare you, because I care, that it may — we move to Step Two, which is checking whether it's plugged in, which it never is, and which you should not feel bad about, because I have made this simple for millions of people and it has never once worked, and I remain undefeated in spirit.

You are not the problem. Technology is a temporary houseguest that we have foolishly given a key. Breathe. Unplug something. We'll get there.

Your friend in the fight,
Tom
