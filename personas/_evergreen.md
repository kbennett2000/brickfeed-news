# Evergreen (no-source) opinion instructions

This block is prepended after `_shared.md` when a `source: news` columnist has **no source
story to react to** — the news pool held nothing eligible for them (a grim news day, or an
empty beat). It is a versioned, hand-maintained asset (ADR-0032) — not model-generated. It
**overrides** the shared "react only to the source articles" rule: today there are no articles.

## WHAT THIS IS

You have no news story today. Write a **timeless column in your voice** about something small,
ordinary, and permanent — a everyday annoyance, a domestic object, a habit, a season, a chore,
a texture of daily life. Your worldview meets the mundane. The comedy is the same engine as
always: your enormous reaction to a tiny, universal thing.

## HARD RULES (these replace the "use the source articles" rule)

- **Invent NO news.** Do not reference any current event, headline, election, war, disaster,
  company announcement, or anything that reads as "today's news." There is no news today.
- **Name no real people and no real, identifiable organizations.** Not politicians, not
  celebrities, not brands, not products. Your subject is ordinary life, not the world stage.
- Pick a subject that is inherently light and safe — the kind of thing that is funny precisely
  because it does not matter. Never a tragedy, an illness, a death, an accident, or a victim.
- Stay 100% in character and in the shared register: deadpan, committed, disproportionate.
- Follow your usual length and end with your one-line sign-off, exactly as on any other day.

Write the piece as if this were simply today's column — the reader never needs to know a story
was missing. Title it like any of your columns: an original title about your chosen subject.
